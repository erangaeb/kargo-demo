**I. Overview**

* Hungary does **not** have mandatory eInvoicing for B2B or B2G transactions in the traditional sense. However, it has implemented a **Real-Time Invoice Reporting (RTIR)** system that effectively mandates electronic reporting of invoice data to the National Tax and Customs Administration (NAV). This system, called **Online Számla**, is the core of Hungary's eInvoicing approach. The goal is to reduce the VAT gap and improve tax collection.

**II. Legislation and Compliance**

* **Key Legislation:**
 * Act CXXVII of 2007 on Value Added Tax: Allows for electronic invoices.
 * Act LXXXIII of 2018: Amends Act CXLIII of 2015 on public procurement, mandating real-time invoice reporting.
 * Directive 2014/55/EU: Transposed, meaning Hungary adheres to the European standard on eInvoicing.
* **Mandatory For:**
 * *Submitting:* All suppliers (economic operators) are required to report invoice data in real-time.
 * *Receiving and Processing:* Central, regional, and local contracting authorities must be able to receive and process eInvoices. Buyer's consent is required.
* **B2B Mandate:** No direct B2B eInvoicing mandate, but RTIR effectively covers B2B transactions.
* **Monitoring Mechanism:** The NAV system serves as the monitoring mechanism.
* **Effective Dates & Updates:**
 * July 1, 2018: RTIR initially implemented for domestic invoices above 100,000 HUF VAT.
 * January 2021: RTIR expanded to cover all B2B and B2C transactions, regardless of amount.
 * April 2021: Mandatory use of NAV Online 3.0.
 * November 2022: NAV Online 3.2 released.
* **Penalty for Non-Compliance:** Fines up to 500,000 HUF (approximately 1,280 EUR) per invoice.

**III. Technical Specifications**

* **Platform:** NAV Online Invoicing System (Online Számla) - a centralized platform.
* **Format:**
 * **NAV XML version 3.0** is the mandatory format for reporting to Online Számla. This XML format is compliant with the European Standard on eInvoicing (EN).
 * While PDF invoices are allowed, they require a digital signature. XML is the recommended format.
* **Digital Signature:** Not required for XML invoices submitted through RTIR. Required for PDF invoices.
* **Information Required in Invoices:**
 * Issuance date
 * Unique sequential number
 * Supplier’s VAT number
 * Supplier’s and customer’s full names and addresses
 * Clear description of goods or services
 * Quantity of goods
 * Date of supply if different from the date of issuance
 * Total taxable value of the supply
 * VAT rate
 * Full details supporting zero VAT
 * Gross value of the invoice
* **Archiving:**
 * Standard: 5 years
 * Accounting purposes: 8 years
 * Archiving abroad is allowed under certain conditions.

**IV. Process for E-invoicing (RTIR) in Hungary**

1. **Registration:** Register on Online Számla. Requires a Hungarian Electronic Government Gate, personal tax number, and business’s tax number.
2. **Technical User:** Create a technical user and keys within the Online Számla system. This allows accounting software to connect to NAV.
3. **ERP Connection:** Connect your ERP or accounting software to the NAV system using the technical user details.
4. **Invoice Creation:** Generate invoices using your software.
5. **Real-Time Reporting:** The software automatically reports the invoice data to the NAV system in real-time in the required XML format.

**V. Affected Parties**

* Companies located in and doing business in and/or out of Hungary.
* All foreign companies with tax residence in Hungary.
* Anyone sending an invoice to domestic taxable persons for transactions in Hungary.
* Non-VAT liable persons or organizations.
* Foreign VAT liable persons.
* Foreign companies with VAT registration in Hungary.

**VI. Key Considerations and Best Practices**

* **Choose Reliable Software:** Select accounting or ERP software that supports the NAV Online Számla system and can generate the required XML format.
* **Partner with a Service Provider:** Consider using an eInvoicing service provider to ensure compliance and streamline the reporting process.
* **Stay Updated:** Keep abreast of changes to the NAV Online Számla system and Hungarian tax regulations.
* **Data Accuracy:** Ensure the accuracy of invoice data to avoid penalties.
* **B2C Invoices:** B2C invoices must not contain personal data (neither name nor address).
* **VAT IDs:** Only one VAT ID (Hungarian, European, or non-European) can be provided per invoice.
