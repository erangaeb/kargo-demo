**Overview:**

Hungary has a mandatory real-time e-invoicing system managed by the National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV), known as Online Számla. The primary goal is to increase VAT transparency and facilitate VAT-related data reporting. The system requires businesses to report invoice data electronically to NAV in real-time.

**Key Features and Requirements:**

* **Mandatory Reporting:** All VAT-registered and resident taxpayers in Hungary are required to comply with the e-invoicing mandate. This applies to both domestic and cross-border transactions.

* **Real-Time Reporting (RTIR):** Real-Time Information Reporting has been in effect since July 1, 2018, initially for domestic invoices with a VAT amount of at least 100,000 HUF. As of April 1, 2021, the mandate expanded to cover all B2C and B2B transactions. This means all invoices must be reported in real-time to the NAV system.

* **Platform:** The official platform for e-invoicing is NAV Online Számla (Online Invoice System).

* **Format:**
 * **Submission to NAV:** Invoices must be submitted to the NAV Online Számla platform in **XML format**. This is the mandatory format for reporting.
 * **Exchange with Buyer/Recipient:** For exchange with the buyer/recipient, invoices can be in **paper, PDF, or XML format**. The recipient does not necessarily need to receive the invoice in the same XML format used for reporting to NAV.

* **Authority:** The National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV) is the governing authority.

* **Legal Basis:** Invoices can be printed or issued electronically as per Act CXXVII of 2007 on Value Added Tax.

**Compliance Requirements:**

* **Registration:** Businesses must register with the NAV Online Számla system.

* **Data Transmission:** Invoice data must be transmitted to the NAV system in real-time.

* **XML Schema:** Adherence to the specific XML schema defined by NAV is crucial for successful data transmission. This schema dictates the structure and content of the XML file.

* **Data Accuracy:** The accuracy of the data reported is paramount. Any discrepancies can lead to penalties.

* **Archiving:** Businesses are responsible for archiving their invoices according to Hungarian regulations.
