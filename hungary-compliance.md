**Executive Summary:**

While e-invoicing itself isn't *mandatory* in Hungary for B2B or B2G transactions, *real-time invoice reporting (RTIR)* to the National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV) is mandatory for nearly all transactions. This is achieved through the NAV Online Számla system, a centralized platform where invoice data must be submitted in XML format. The system is designed for real-time VAT data collection and anti-fraud measures. Compliance is crucial, as penalties for non-compliance can be significant.

**1. Legislation and Regulatory Framework:**

* **Responsible Authority:** National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV).
* **Legal Basis:** Act CXXVII of 2007 on Value Added Tax allows for electronic invoices.
* **EU Directive Transposition:** Directive 2014/55/EU on electronic invoicing in public procurement has been transposed.
* **B2B Mandate:** No mandatory e-invoicing for B2B.
* **Monitoring Mechanism:** Real-time reporting to NAV acts as a monitoring mechanism.
* **European Standard:** The European standard on e-invoicing (EN) is fully implemented.

**2. NAV Online Számla System:**

* **Centralized Platform:** All invoices must be reported live and electronically to NAV.
* **Real-time Reporting (RTIR):**
 * Introduced in July 2018 for invoices with VAT exceeding 100,000 HUF.
 * Expanded in April 2021 to include virtually all B2B and B2C transactions, regardless of amount.
* **NAV Online 3.0 (and later versions):** Mandatory from June 1, 2021. Requires exclusive reporting of XML files to NAV. Version 3.2 (November 2022) includes error validations and technical cancellations.
* **Affected Parties:**
 * VAT-registered businesses in Hungary.
 * Foreign companies with VAT registration in Hungary.
 * Non-VAT liable persons.
* **Exemptions:** Foreign distance sellers joining the OSS regime in July 2021 are exempt from RTIR.

**3. Technical Requirements and Format Guides:**

* **Format:**
 * **Mandatory for NAV Reporting:** XML (NAV XML version 3.0 or later). This is the *only* accepted format for reporting to the NAV system.
 * **For Invoice Exchange (B2B/B2G):** While XML is compliant with the European Standard, paper, PDF, or XML can be used for exchange with buyers.
* **Digital Signature:** Not required for XML submissions to NAV. Required if sending an invoice in PDF format.
* **Information Required on Invoices (for reporting):**
 * Issuance date
 * Unique sequential number
 * Supplier’s VAT number
 * Supplier’s and customer’s full names and addresses
 * Description of goods or services
 * Quantity of goods
 * Date of supply if different from the date of issuance
 * Total taxable value of the supply
 * VAT rate
 * Full details supporting zero VAT
 * Gross value of the invoice
* **Customer Info Field:** Must differentiate between invoices sent to Hungary, EU, or non-EU countries. Only one VAT ID (Hungarian, European, or non-European) can be used.
* **B2C Invoices:** Must *not* contain personal data (neither name nor address).

**4. Compliance Requirements and Penalties:**

* **Mandatory Reporting:** Real-time reporting to NAV is mandatory for almost all transactions.
* **Penalty for Non-Compliance:** Fine of up to 500,000 HUF (approximately 1,280 EUR) per invoice for unreported or incorrectly reported invoices.

**5. Process for Reporting to NAV:**

1. **Register on Online Számla:** Requires a Hungarian Electronic Government Gate, personal tax number, and business’s tax number.
2. **Create a Technical User and Keys:** This allows accounting software to connect to the NAV system.
3. **Connect ERP to the NAV System:** Enter technical user details in invoicing software.
4. **Create and Issue an Invoice:** Generate new invoices with invoicing software.
5. **Report the Electronic Invoice:** Report in real-time and in XML format to NAV.

**6. Archiving:**

* **Standard:** 5 years.
* **Accounting Purposes:** 8 years.
* **Archiving Abroad:** Allowed under certain conditions.

**7. Key Takeaways and Recommendations:**

* **Focus on Real-time Reporting:** The core requirement is accurate and timely reporting to NAV.
* **XML is Essential:** Mastering the NAV XML format is critical.
* **Choose Reliable Software:** Select accounting or e-invoicing software that supports NAV XML generation and real-time reporting.
* **Stay Updated:** Monitor NAV announcements for changes to the system and regulations.
* **Consider a Service Provider:** Partnering with an e-invoicing service provider can simplify compliance and reduce the risk of errors.

**8. Additional Information:**

* **SAF-T:** While not currently required, SAF-T reporting may be introduced in the future.
* **EKAER:** The Electronic Public Road Trade Control System (EKAER) is used for reporting risky goods transported by road.
