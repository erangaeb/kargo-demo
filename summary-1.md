Okay, here's a detailed summary of e-invoicing in Hungary, compiled from all the provided sources.  This aims to be a comprehensive guide, covering legislation, technical details, compliance, and practical steps.

**I. Overview**

*   **E-Invoicing Status:** E-invoicing itself is *not mandatory* in Hungary for B2B or B2G transactions. However, *real-time invoice reporting (RTIR)* to the National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV) is mandatory for virtually all transactions. This effectively makes electronic data submission a necessity.
*   **RTIR Purpose:** The RTIR system, implemented through the NAV Online Számla platform, is primarily an anti-VAT fraud measure. It allows NAV to monitor transactions in real-time and collect VAT-related data.
*   **NAV Online Számla:** This is the central platform for reporting invoice data.  It requires data transmission in XML format.
*   **Directive 2014/55/EU:** Transposed into Hungarian law, mandating acceptance and processing of e-invoices by central, regional, and local contracting authorities.

**II. Legislation and Regulatory Body**

*   **Responsible Authority:** National Tax and Customs Administration of Hungary (NAV) (Nemzeti Adó és Vámhivatal).
*   **Key Legislation:**
    *   Act CXXVII of 2007 on Value Added Tax (VAT Act):  Forms the basis for allowing electronic invoices.
    *   Real-time Invoice Reporting (RTIR) regulations, initially introduced in July 2018 and expanded since.
*   **Governing Entity:** Hungarian Tax Authority (NAV) manages e-invoicing through the Online Számla platform.

**III. Key Requirements and Scope**

*   **Affected Parties:**
    *   Companies located in and doing business in and/or out of Hungary.
    *   All foreign companies with tax residence in Hungary.
    *   Anyone sending an invoice to domestic taxable persons for transactions in Hungary.
    *   Non-VAT liable persons or organizations.
    *   Foreign VAT liable persons.
    *   Foreign companies with VAT registration in Hungary.
*   **Transactions Covered:**
    *   B2B (Business-to-Business)
    *   B2C (Business-to-Consumer)
    *   Export transactions
    *   Intra-EU transactions
    *   All outgoing transactions
*   **Documents to be Reported:**
    *   Invoices
    *   Credit notes
    *   Debit notes

**IV. Technical Specifications**

*   **Format:**
    *   **Mandatory for Reporting:** NAV XML version 3.0 (or later versions like 3.2) for e-invoicing via Online Számla. This is the *only* accepted format for reporting to NAV.
    *   **For Invoice Exchange (not reporting):** While XML is recommended, paper, PDF, or XML can be used for the actual exchange of invoices between parties.
*   **Digital Signature:** A digital signature is *not required* for XML invoices submitted to NAV.  It *is* mandatory if you send an invoice in PDF format.
*   **Data Fields Required (for the invoice itself):**
    *   Issuance date
    *   Unique sequential number
    *   Supplier’s VAT number
    *   Supplier’s and customer’s full names and addresses
    *   Clear description of goods or services
    *   Quantity of goods
    *   Date of supply if different from the date of issuance
    *   Total taxable value of the supply
    *   VAT rate
    *   Full details supporting zero VAT
    *   Gross value of the invoice
*   **Platform:**
    *   **RTIR:** Online Számla (centralized platform).
    *   **E-Invoice:** No specific infrastructure mandated for the actual exchange of invoices.

**V. Reporting Process (Step-by-Step)**

1.  **Register on Online Számla:** Requires a Hungarian Electronic Government Gate, personal tax number, and business’s tax number.
2.  **Create a Technical User and Keys:** This allows your accounting software to connect to the NAV system.
3.  **Connect ERP to the NAV System:** Enter the technical user details into your invoicing software.
4.  **Create and Issue an Invoice:** Generate new invoices using your software.
5.  **Report the Electronic Invoice:** Report the invoice data to NAV in real-time and in the required XML format.

**VI. Archiving**

*   **Standard Archiving Period:** 5 years.
*   **Archiving Period for Accounting Purposes:** 8 years.
*   **Archiving Abroad:** Allowed under certain conditions.

**VII. Compliance and Penalties**

*   **Penalty for Non-Compliance:** A fine of up to 500,000 HUF (approximately 1,280 EUR) per invoice for unreported or incorrectly reported invoices.

**VIII. Updates and Changes**

*   **NAV Online 3.0:** Mandatory from 1 June 2021.
*   **Version 3.17:** Updated on March 31, 2022, includes error validations and browser limitations.
*   **Version 3.2:** Released on November 2, 2022, includes technical cancellations and personal data display changes.

**IX. How to Prepare for E-Invoicing in Hungary**

*   **Source Reliable Accounting Software:** Choose software that supports the creation of the required XML format and integration with the NAV Online Számla system.  This will minimize manual input and potential errors.
*   **Partner with a Reliable E-Invoicing Service Provider:** Consider using a service provider to ensure compliance with the latest regulations and streamline the reporting process.
*   **Register for the NAV System:** Visit NAV's offices or website for assistance with registration.

**X. Additional Information**

*   **VAT Gap Reduction:** Hungary has significantly reduced its VAT gap since 2013, partly due to the implementation of RTIR.
*   **Draft VAT Returns:** The Tax Authority can propose draft VAT returns based on VAT data collected through the Online Számla system.
*   **EKAER:** Electronic reporting for risky goods transported by road.
*   **SAF-T:** Not currently required but expected in the future.

**XI. Key Takeaways**

*   While e-invoicing is not strictly mandatory, the mandatory real-time reporting requirements effectively necessitate electronic data submission.
*   Compliance with the NAV Online Számla system and the required XML format is crucial.
*   Staying updated on the latest regulations and platform versions is essential.
*   Consider using accounting software or a service provider to simplify the process and ensure compliance.
