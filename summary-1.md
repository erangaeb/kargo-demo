Hungary mandates real-time invoice reporting (RTIR) to the National Tax and Customs Administration (NAV) via the Online Számla platform. This applies to all VAT-registered businesses for both B2B and B2C transactions. The required format is NAV XML. While e-invoicing itself is voluntary, real-time reporting is mandatory. Invoices must be archived for 8 years for accounting purposes (5 years standard). Non-compliance can result in fines up to 500,000 HUF.
